## 🏢 About Us (Company Profile)

**Shallom Prefab Systems** is a dynamic, rapidly growing manufacturer, exporter, and turnkey service provider specializing in advanced prefabricated construction solutions. Established with industry roots dating back to **2009** at Delhi (India) and formally GST-registered/incorporated in **2017**, the company has built a stellar reputation for delivering high-quality, durable, aesthetically versatile, and rapidly deployable structures across India.

Under the visionary leadership of Proprietor **Mr. Arvind Kumar Gautam**, Shallom Prefab Systems caters to diverse sectors including residential housing, hospitality & tourism, education, corporate offices, healthcare, and heavy infrastructure/mining industries.

### Mission & Commitment
- Deliver international quality standards in prefabricated engineering.
- Enable sustainable, eco-friendly, and energy-efficient building infrastructure.
- Provide end-to-end turnkey execution from architectural design to on-site commissioning.

## 🏗️ Core Business Verticals & Product Offerings

### 1. Residential & Hospitality Solutions
- **Prefabricated Luxurious Houses & Villas:** Premium architectural finishes, custom layouts, and energy-efficient living.
- **Modular Cottages & Wooden Houses:** Aesthetic, nature-integrated cottages tailored for eco-resorts, hill stations, and holiday homes.
- **Farmhouses & Rooftop Extensions:** Lightweight, structurally sound living spaces erected directly on terraces or rural plots.
- **Resorts & Recreation Blocks:** Fast-track guest houses, reception lounges, and leisure spaces.

### 2. Commercial & Corporate Infrastructure
- **Prefabricated Site & Executive Offices:** Modern workspace cabins, manager/director suites, and multi-desk collaborative offices.
- **Office Cafeterias & Canteens:** Hygienic dining halls, modern food service spaces, and pantry modules.
- **Security Guard Cabins & Toll Plazas:** Heavy-duty, weather-tight security booths, entrance checkpoints, and toll booths.
- **Commercial Retail Outlets & Kiosks:** Modular store fronts and inner-side commercial setups for shopping complexes.

### 3. Industrial & Heavy Infrastructure Utilities
- **Labor Camps & Hutments:** Safe, modular worker accommodations engineered specifically for large-scale mining and construction sites.
- **Industrial Sheds & PEB Steel Buildings:** Large-span pre-engineered steel structures for warehouses, workshops, and factories.
- **Modular Sanitation & Portable Toilets:** Mobile toilet blocks, shower cubicles, and eco-friendly sanitary units with plumbing integration.
- **Bunk Houses & Mobile Site Storage:** Containerized and modular field offices, emergency shelters, and equipment storage units.

### 4. Specialized Interior Contracting & Turnkey Services
- **Prefab Building Installation & Maintenance Services:** End-to-end site erection, lifecycle repair, and scheduled maintenance.
- **Dry Wall Partition & Ceiling Works:** Acoustic suspended ceilings, lightweight dry walls, and thermal insulation panelling.
- **Thermal & Acoustic Insulation:** Advanced PUF/Aerocon panelling solutions to cut HVAC energy bills and sound transmission.

## 🌟 Key Advantages of Shallom Prefab Systems

- 🚀 **Rapid Construction Speed:** Pre-engineered off-site in controlled factory conditions; cuts standard building project timelines drastically.
- 🪶 **Lightweight yet Robust:** Minimizes foundation dead-load requirements without compromising structural rigidity.
- 📐 **Lesser Footprint Area:** Slimmer walling systems maximize usable carpet area compared to traditional thick masonry.
- 🔄 **Modular & Future Expansion:** Easily scale, add stories, expand footprints, or reconfigure interior partitions as needs evolve.
- 🚚 **100% Relocatable / Portable:** Structures can be dismantled, transported, and reassembled at new sites with minimal loss of materials.
- 🌡️ **Superior Energy Efficiency:** Utilizes high-density PUF insulation panels, keeping interiors naturally temperate and drastically cutting electricity consumption.
- 🛡️ **Weather, Wind & Seismic Resistant:** Engineered to withstand extreme atmospheric conditions, heavy monsoons, and high seismic vibrations (Zone-V certified designs).
- 🔥 **Zero Fire Hazards:** Fire-retardant materials compliant with international safety standards.
- 🌿 **Environment Friendly & Recyclable:** Sustainable construction approach producing zero site pollution and minimal carbon footprint.
- 💰 **Low Lifecycle Maintenance:** Galvanized, pre-painted anti-corrosive sheets eliminate frequent repainting or structural upkeep.
